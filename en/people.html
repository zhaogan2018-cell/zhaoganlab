Gan Zhao

Dr. Gan Zhao completed his PhD through joint training at Huazhong Agricultural University and the Andrew Vaughan laboratory at the University of Pennsylvania, followed by postdoctoral training in the Andrew Vaughan and Edward Morrisey laboratories. In March 2025, he joined the School of Medicine at Xiamen University and established his laboratory to investigate the cellular and molecular basis of lung injury and repair.
