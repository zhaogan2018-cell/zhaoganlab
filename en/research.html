Research

Decoding the cellular logic of lung injury and regeneration.

01

Pulmonary vascular remodeling and tissue homeostasis

Building on our studies of COUP-TF2 and TGF-βR2 in vascular repair, we will investigate vascular cell-state transitions, coordinated vascular–epithelial remodeling and the restoration of tissue homeostasis during organ regeneration.

Science Advances · 2020 ↗ · Science Translational Medicine · 2024 ↗
02

Immune microenvironments in lung regeneration and maladaptive repair

Building on our work on endothelial SPARCL1 and macrophage activation, we will examine spatial and temporal immune–vascular–epithelial interactions, the transition from inflammation resolution to regeneration, and mechanisms underlying maladaptive repair.

Nature Communications · 2024 ↗
03

Regulatory networks and precision regenerative therapies

Drawing on collaborative lung regeneration studies and targeted delivery research, we will integrate cell states, spatial tissue information and functional validation to identify regulatory nodes of effective regeneration and explore cell- and repair-stage-specific regenerative therapies.

Science · 2024 ↗ · PNAS · 2024 ↗ · Nature Nanotechnology · 2025 ↗
