01 / OUR LAB

Investigating the biology of lung recovery.

The Gan Zhao Lab is based at the School of Medicine, Xiamen University, with a research focus on the response of pulmonary endothelial, epithelial, and immune cells to injury and their coordinated roles in tissue regeneration.

Our work integrates experimental models, lineage tracing, single-cell approaches, and molecular perturbation to uncover mechanisms that may inform new therapeutic strategies for respiratory disease.
